Quick Reference Guide

TURN // ECONOMY

Your Turn

Choose:

  • Standard: 1 Action + 1 Maneuver
  • Spend 2 Strain: 1 Action + 2 Maneuvers
  • Trade Your Action: 2 Maneuvers + (no Action)

Maneuver Limit: Normally, you may perform no more than 2 Maneuvers during a single turn unless a talent or specific rule says otherwise.

COMBAT // RANGE

Base Attack Difficulty

Engaged — Brawl / MeleeAverage
Short — RangedEasy
Medium — RangedAverage
Long — RangedHard
Extreme — RangedDaunting

Ranged (Light) at engaged increases difficulty by 1; Ranged (Heavy) increases it by 2; Gunnery cannot be used while engaged.

MANEUVERS // COMMON

Common Maneuvers

  • Aim: improve the next combat check.
  • Assist: help another character's next action.
  • Guarded Stance: trade offensive ease for melee defense.
  • Take Cover: gain ranged defense.
  • Manage Gear: draw, ready, holster, load, retrieve, or stow.
  • Move: change position or range bands.
DICE // RESULTS

Success Failure Advantage Threat Triumph Despair

ENVIRONMENT // FALLING

Fall Damage

Short10 damage + 10 strain
Medium30 damage + 20 strain
LongIncapacitated + Critical Injury (+50) + 30 strain
ExtremeIncapacitated + Critical Injury (+75) + 40 strain

Break the fall: make an Average Athletics or Coordination check. Each Success reduces damage by 1; each Advantage reduces strain by 1. A Triumph may reduce the fall by one range band at the GM’s discretion. Soak reduces the wound damage from a fall, but not the strain.

DESTINY // LIGHT & DARK

Spending Destiny Points

Players spend Light Side GM spends Dark Side
Upgrade your checkUpgrade one Ability die to Proficiency.
Raise difficultyUpgrade one Difficulty die to Challenge on an opposing check.
Story detailIntroduce a plausible helpful complication, item, feature, or fact with GM approval.
AbilitiesActivate talents, Force powers, or effects that specifically require a Destiny Point.

After spending: flip the token to the opposite side, making it available to the other side. On a single check, the acting side and the opposing side may each spend a Destiny Point.

STARSHIP // TURN

Vehicle Essentials

Starship actions include piloting support, jamming, shields, scanning, damage control, hyperspace, firing weapons, and spoofing missiles. Pilot maneuvers cover speed, movement, evasive maneuvers, staying on target, Punch It, angle shields, and terrain navigation.

ASTROGATION // ROUTES

Astrogation Flow

  1. Determine the route's plotting difficulty.
  2. Add route, destination, ship-condition, and situational modifiers.
  3. Apply Astrogation talents and other effects.
  4. Resolve the check and apply travel-time / mishap results.
